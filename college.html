How learning works

A clear journey from application to completion.

The exact structure varies by programme, but students can expect a guided process that combines orientation, learning, practical work and formal assessment.

1

Apply & confirm eligibility

Choose a course, submit your details and supporting documents, and receive confirmation of programme-specific entry requirements.

2

Orientation & study plan

Understand the timetable, learning outcomes, assessment requirements, student support and how to use the student portal.

3

Theory & guided learning

Build core knowledge through facilitated lessons, learning material, discussion and structured activities.

4

Practical & evidence

Demonstrate skills through practical exercises, simulations and, where required, workplace or portfolio evidence.

5

Assessment & completion

Complete the required assessments and administrative steps before final results and programme completion are confirmed.

Graduation pathway

What do I need to complete before I graduate?

Graduation and certification are based on the requirements of the programme you register for. Your facilitator and Admissions team will confirm the exact requirements for your intake.

✓
Complete required learning activitiesFinish the modules, classwork and learning activities assigned to your programme.
✓
Demonstrate practical competenceComplete the required skills demonstrations, simulations or practical evidence.
✓
Meet assessment requirementsAchieve competence in the assessments, projects or portfolio components required by the programme.
✓
Meet attendance and administration requirementsMaintain the required participation and ensure programme documentation and administrative requirements are complete.
✓
Receive final completion confirmationOnce all programme requirements are satisfied, results and eligibility for the relevant completion or certification process are confirmed.
Programme rules differ. Afromed Admissions will provide the official requirements, duration, assessment model and certification information for the specific course and intake you select.
