Privacy Notice

Afromed Development (registered South African NPO 245-061) respects personal privacy and uses personal information for legitimate correspondence and partnership enquiries. This notice describes how the public website works and how to contact us about your information.

Information you choose to send

The partnership enquiry form prepares an email message in your own email application. Information you enter may include your name, work email address, organisation, country, interests and message. The website itself does not save the form fields in a website database or transmit them to our server. You decide whether to send the resulting email.

If you send an email to us, it is processed through your email provider and ours. We use the contents to respond to your enquiry and consider potential cooperation. Please do not send personal medical records, health information or other sensitive details through this form.

Website technology and external services

The current site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run behavioural advertising analytics. It loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Our hosting provider may maintain normal security and access logs. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' privacy notices.

Information handling and retention

Correspondence may be kept for as long as it is needed for the enquiry, agreed relationship, lawful obligations or legitimate organisational recordkeeping. Only authorised personnel should access partnership communications. We do not publish your contact details or sell enquiry information to advertisers.

Your privacy rights

You may contact us to ask what information we hold, request a correction or raise a concern about how your details are handled. Applicable rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) will be respected.
