Who we are

A South African heart. A vision without borders.

Afromed Development is a registered non-profit organisation based in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Our work is grounded in the belief that stronger communities are built through access to healthcare, practical knowledge and responsive support.

With roots in emergency healthcare, we bring a practical, community-focused perspective to prevention, education, outreach and human development.

Proud sponsor & organiser

Afromed Development proudly sponsors and organises the South African Emergency Medical Services Awards — a national programme created to recognise the dedication, courage and excellence of emergency medical professionals, teams and organisations.

The SA EMS Awards platform brings attention to the everyday heroes behind emergency response and creates opportunities for meaningful institutional and corporate participation.

How we work

Credibility is built into the work.

We approach partnership development through community relevance, responsible governance and openness about results. We believe trust must be earned through delivery, not grand claims.
