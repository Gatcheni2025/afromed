THE HOLDING COMPANY

Strategic integration.
Measurable community impact.

Afromed.life is the strategic holding company that unites three specialised operating entities to deliver end-to-end emergency healthcare solutions across KwaZulu-Natal. Established in 2012 and headquartered in Durban's Avoca Hills, we combine 24/7 advanced life support operations, HWSETA-accredited professional training, and targeted community development under one values-driven group.

Our Mission

To deliver integrated, high-quality emergency medical care, build professional healthcare capacity through accredited training, and drive sustainable community health development across KwaZulu-Natal — leaving no community behind.

Our Vision

A KwaZulu-Natal where every community has rapid access to life-saving care, skilled local responders, and proactive health support. A province where young people see healthcare as a viable, dignified career and communities are active partners in their own wellbeing.

Our Values
• Clinical Excellence & Patient Safety First
• Youth Empowerment & Local Capacity
• Integrity, Accountability & Transparency
FOUNDED BY VISIONARIES

Young Black entrepreneurs building generational impact in KZN healthcare.

Sthembiso Emmanuel Ngubo and Nelisiwe Gloria Ngcobo established Afromed in 2012 with a clear purpose: to combine world-class clinical standards with deep community roots and a commitment to developing the next generation of KZN healthcare professionals.
